What it does

A memory for everything
on your screen.

You read hundreds of pages, chats, and docs a day, and hear hours of calls. OpenRewind keeps all of it, so the thing you half remember is always one search away.

Search what you heard

Your mic and your computer’s audio are transcribed by Whisper on your own machine. Meetings and videos show up in the same search as screen text.

Pick the apps it should never see, like password managers, banking and private browsing. They’re blurred in every screenshot and their text is never saved.

How it works

Three quiet steps,
all on your machine.

01

Capture

Every 2 seconds OpenRewind takes a snapshot, and throws it away if nothing changed. Mic and system audio are saved in 30-second chunks, and silence is dropped.

02

Understand

Text is pulled from the focused window, falling back to on-device OCR. Audio is transcribed by local Whisper. No server ever sees it.

03

Remember

Everything lands in a full-text index on your disk. Search it from the timeline or the menu bar.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is OpenRewind really free?

Yes. There’s no trial, no paid plan, and no account. It’s open source under the MIT license, so you can use it, share it, and change it however you like.

Does any of my data leave my computer?

No. Screenshots, audio, transcripts, and the search index all stay in one folder on your disk. Speech is turned into text on your own computer, and the built-in search page can only be opened from your own computer.

How much disk space does it use?

Frames that didn’t change are skipped and silent audio is dropped, which keeps it small. You can also have older recordings deleted automatically after a day, week, month, or year.

What won’t it record?

Password fields, the lock screen and the screen saver are never recorded. The first time you open OpenRewind it asks which apps to block, with password managers, money apps, private browsing windows and bank websites already ticked. Blocked apps are blurred wherever they are on screen and their text is never saved, and you can change the list anytime in Settings → Blocked Apps & Sites. You can also pause screen or audio recording from the menu.

Is this the old Rewind app?

No. OpenRewind is new, independent code inspired by the idea of rewinding your screen. It isn’t made by or connected to Rewind AI, Limitless, or Meta.

Can I contribute?

Please do. Issues, ideas, and pull requests are all welcome on GitHub.
